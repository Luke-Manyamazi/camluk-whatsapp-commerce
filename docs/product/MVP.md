# Camluk WhatsApp Commerce MVP

## Vision

Bring Camluk Technologies' services to WhatsApp and turn customer conversations into qualified leads, service requests, and business opportunities.

## Core Services

- Web Development
- Business Software
- AI & Automation
- Cloud & Deployment

## First Customer

Camluk Technologies

## MVP Goal

A customer should be able to message Camluk on WhatsApp, understand our services, describe a business problem, receive useful guidance, request a quote or consultation, and be handed over to a human when necessary.
